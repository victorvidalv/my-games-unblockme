# Personajes de historia

Cinco ilustraciones individuales generadas con la herramienta de imágenes integrada. Referencias directas de estilo: los retratos originales de Jean, Lía y Van, y la escena final de Van. Contornos oscuros gruesos, sombras planas, formas angulares y textura de papel impreso.

Archivos finales en `public/historia`: `vhorn.webp`, `kogn.webp`, `sahrim.webp`, `ennari.webp` y `aluen.webp`. Versiones WebP optimizadas a 640 × 960 px, calidad 72. Los originales de alta resolución se conservan en el historial de imágenes generadas.

## vhorn.webp

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one standalone portrait narrative character illustration with an integrated background for the existing fantasy adventure game.
Input images: images 1, 2 and 3 are the ORIGINAL game hero portraits, authoritative DRAWING STYLE references only. Image 4 is a finished scene from the same game, a reference for how to apply the hero drawing style to an integrated character-and-environment illustration. Do not copy any reference character's identity, hair, clothes, props, face or pose.
CRITICAL VISUAL CONSISTENCY: Draw as the SAME ARTIST as the original game heroes. Bold thick angular dark ink contours, expressive graphic anime/manga face construction, strongly grouped hair and cloth folds, hard-edged flat cel shadows, limited printed colors, rough ink edges, subtle distressed vintage parchment texture. Clear readable forms and confident thick lines, medium amount of detail. No realism, no semirealism, no glossy 3D, no soft painterly art, no thin delicate engraving. This should immediately belong beside Jean, Lía and Van in the existing game.
Composition: Portrait 2:3 full-bleed OPAQUE illustration, ONE named main character only, three-quarter body large in the foreground with face near upper third, both hands and signature props readable, integrated setting clearly visible around them. Complete separate illustration, not a collage, contact sheet or character sheet. No text, lettering, labels, names, logos, watermark, border or UI. Rich but uncluttered scene; no extra protagonists.
Primary request: VHORN, THE EXTINGUISHER, the elegant threatening villain. A tall extraordinarily slender humanoid figure made of shadow and a star-filled night sky. Long tapered silhouette, dark flowing ceremonial coat whose interior contains sparse cold stars, inky smoky edges but solid graphic shape. A broken crown of angular BLACK GLASS shards sits on his head. His face is almost featureless deep shadow with two SMALL COLD WHITE EYES, no grin, no mouth full of teeth, no skull. Dignified, quiet, ominous and elegant.
Mandatory signature prop: A GIANT ANTIQUE BRASS CANDLE SNUFFER, a long slender staff with a clearly visible LARGE UPSIDE-DOWN BELL-SHAPED METAL CUP at the end, designed to cover and extinguish candles. It is NOT a scythe, NOT a spear, NOT a sword. Hold it diagonally across his body, the bell hovering over a small candle in the lower foreground, a thin wisp of smoke showing the flame has just gone out. The brass must be readable against the dark figure.
Backdrop: A silent nighttime stone terrace above distant darkened fantasy roofs, extinguished candles, deep petrol-teal and midnight navy sky, a few stars. A muted moonlight rim outlines the shadow figure and glass crown. Antique brass and parchment highlights. Threatening but suitable for a general-audience fantasy story, absolutely NO gore or violence. Strong clear silhouette, white eyes, broken black glass crown and gigantic brass snuffer all prominently visible.
